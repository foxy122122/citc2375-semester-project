Project demonstration

The project demonstration is a required part of Exam 1 and is worth 25 points.

The purpose of the demonstration is to show that you can explain and demonstrate the project you created. This is a technical walkthrough, not a formal speech or professionally edited presentation.

Create a short demonstration that shows your deployed website, demonstrates its current functionality, and highlights selected portions of your code.

Choose one of the following formats.
Option A: Video demonstration

Create a screen recording approximately 4–6 minutes long. Show your deployed website and relevant portions of your project in Visual Studio Code.

Explain the project through narration or clear on-screen annotations. You do not need to appear on camera, use a webcam, or professionally edit the recording.

Video hosting and submission:

    Upload your completed recording to a video-hosting service such as YouTube or Vimeo.

Do not upload the video file itself to Brightspace or include it in your project ZIP. Submit the video link as described above.
Option B: Document or slideshow

Create a short Word document, PowerPoint presentation, or PDF containing screenshots of your deployed website and selected code snippets.

You do not need to write an essay or create a professionally designed presentation.

Save your demonstration as Exam1_Demonstration_YourName using the appropriate file extension (.docx, .pptx, or .pdf). Include this file in your project ZIP.
